Simple Workflow

Step 1: Scan

Use your scanner to capture documents as PDFs. OCR support pulls text automatically for easy handling.

Step 2: Enter Details

Open Alinof Archives, view the scan on-screen, and fill supplier, description, date, and amount fields. Drag text directly from the PDF.

Step 3: Save and Index

Hit save. The document is indexed in your personal database, ready for instant retrieval.

Step 4: Search and Act

Query by any detail. Preview, print, compare, or export with one click.
